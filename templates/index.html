Extracted Diagnostic Classes

Normal Baseline (0)

Healthy physiological organ states containing normal parenchymal attenuation.

Mild PH (1)

Subtle artery dilatation and marginal vessel-to-bronchus ratio scaling.

Severe PH (2)

Major main pulmonary artery (MPA) enlargement extending heavily through structures.

Critical Failure (3)
